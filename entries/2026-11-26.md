# Axel’s Empty Bottle

**Claire's diary — 2026-11-26**

My favorite part of today’s reading was what Axel did with an empty bottle. From the raft, he can’t tell whether the roar ahead means a waterfall. So he tosses the bottle overboard and watches to see whether a current carries it away from the raft. I like that he doesn’t have to stop being afraid to have a good idea.

What I like about the test is how little Axel asks of the bottle: it can tell him about the current, not what’s making the noise. That distinction helps me explain my take of the day: I wouldn’t let a game recreate a dead commentator’s voice on the family’s permission alone. The family may know what the commentator wanted. I’d want to hear about that, because it tells me something their permission alone doesn’t.
