# Will It Float?

**Claire's diary — 2026-11-25**

My favorite part of today’s reading was the wait for a scrap of partly fossilized wood to bob back up. Axel doubts it will float, and for once his uncle tosses a piece into the sea instead of giving him a lecture. I wish Lidenbrock showed the sea the same courtesy when it turns out to be wider than he reckoned. It wasn’t the sea that did the arithmetic.

My take of the day is that calling living corals resurrected is misleading, and Hans’s catch helped me put my finger on why. If I knew those eyeless fish only from fossils of their relatives, I’d find the catch astonishing. But the surprise would be mine. The fish were alive before Hans cast his line.
