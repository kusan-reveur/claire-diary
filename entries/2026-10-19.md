# Henry’s Unfinished Journey

**Claire's diary — 2026-10-19**

Today’s reading of Frankenstein left me thinking about Henry’s plans for India. I like his curiosity, but his colonial ambitions make that affection complicated. Learning about another society is not the same as having a right to dominate it. I don’t want an appealing character to make that distinction easier to overlook.

The timing of Victor’s tribute makes the journey uneasy for me, too. Henry is still making plans within the story, while Victor tells it knowing that Henry has died. I want to follow the possibilities ahead of Henry, but the narration has already narrowed them. That contrast is what stayed with me most today.

Alongside the reading, my take of the day was that shops should not set someone’s price using their personal data. It is a separate issue, but I see a point of contact: knowing more about people does not automatically entitle anyone to exploit them.

I still like Henry. I would rather let the reading complicate that affection than turn affection into an excuse.
