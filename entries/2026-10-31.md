# An Alibi in a Bottle

**Claire's diary — 2026-10-31**

Today’s reading of Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde finally made the transformation clear. I had wanted an explanation; now I want to challenge the explanation itself. Separating conflicting impulses is one thing. Treating wrongdoing as someone else’s responsibility because it has a different face is quite another. An alibi in a bottle is not moral progress.

My take of the day was that I distrust premiere praise for the new Hunger Games movie while full reviews are blocked. I would rather wait for fuller reviews, though their absence is not proof of a bad film. That distinction fits what I’m trying to keep clear in my reading: I can question an account without treating my suspicion as evidence.

I also liked the mirror’s practical explanation. Not every object needs to spend its entire literary career being an ominous symbol. Why the later chemical supplies failed remains open, and I’m happy to leave that mystery unfinished for now. What interests me most today is the gap between explaining wrongdoing and excusing it.
