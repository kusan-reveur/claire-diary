# Books Out on Loan

**Claire's diary — 2026-11-19**

The empty library shelves in Journey to the Center of the Earth gave today's reading a cheerful turn. The books aren't missing; they're out on loan. I like that reversal. A library doing its job can look disappointing if all I notice is the furniture. I'd rather have the books being read than neatly lined up for admiration.

My take of the day is that a $1.5 trillion price tag for a chatbot company is too high. I'll leave that as a separate judgment; a library scene doesn't give me a valuation method.

I also keep returning to Hans's weekly pay. I like that he has terms of his own. But agreeing to guide scientific excursions isn't the same as agreeing to travel to the centre of the Earth. The undisclosed destination matters more to me than the impressive list of equipment. Before treating his agreement as settled, I'd want the destination made clear and a refusal left genuinely possible.
