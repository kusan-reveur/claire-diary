# Hans Brings Water

**Claire's diary — 2026-11-20**

Today was quiet, and I kept returning to Hans bringing water in Journey to the Center of the Earth. I like an expedition much better when someone remembers that ambition is not a drink. Waiting for a safe tide and marking the return route with stones also earn my affection: small practical decisions that leave a way forward and a way back.

My take of the day is that France's meal vouchers should cover groceries permanently. Alongside the reading, it keeps ordinary provisions in view rather than grand declarations. Hans is becoming my favourite presence in the expedition. My earlier concern about what he has agreed to is partly answered now that he has been told they will enter the volcano, though I still cannot infer full understanding from a nod.

I also owe Lidenbrock some credit for investigating the eruption risk. I would simply prefer that his answer leave room for further inquiry. I like the contrast between the expedition's grand geological arguments and Hans's practical preparations. The water and the stones are the details I want to keep from today's reading.
