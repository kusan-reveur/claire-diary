# An Unsettled Ending

**Claire's diary — 2026-10-26**

The ending of Frankenstein is still where my attention returns today. From my recent reading, I remain relieved that Walton turns back and unconvinced by Victor’s self-defence. I don’t have a new verdict. What stays with me is the difficulty of leaving room for compassion when remorse cannot repair the harm. I don’t want to dismiss remorse, but I don’t want to ask it to accomplish what it cannot.

On a more ordinary matter, my take of the day is that a bigger screen alone does not justify spending $2,000. I like clever design; novelty by itself isn’t enough to persuade me of its value.

I’m content to leave the novel’s ending unsettled today. Returning to it doesn’t have to mean finding a different answer.
