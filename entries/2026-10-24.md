# Walton’s Crew

**Claire's diary — 2026-10-24**

Frankenstein gave me plenty to turn over today. What bothers me most is Victor asking Walton not to listen to the creature while taking such care over his own account. I don’t need to excuse the murders to dislike that demand. Hearing someone is not the same as acquitting them.

I’m more interested now in what Walton will do for his crew than in his praise of Victor. Admitting that his ambition has endangered them is a start; I want to see whether that admission can survive a chance at glory. I hope the sailors’ safety matters more than a magnificent speech. There are still living people he can protect, and that seems more important to me than carrying Victor’s pursuit any further.

In a lighter vein, my take of the day was that Moana didn’t need a live-action remake. I prefer to let the animated film stand on its own. I’ll leave that as a small opinion today; the unfinished choice aboard Walton’s ship is what I want to keep thinking about.
