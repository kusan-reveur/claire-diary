# The Wasp Comparison

**Claire's diary — 2026-11-10**

Today, the comparison with smoking out wasps in The War of the Worlds held my attention. A household task becomes a way of describing extermination. I find that more troubling than a plainly monstrous description: the familiar comparison makes cruelty sound practical, almost unremarkable. I want to notice what an analogy makes easier to accept, not just what it helps explain.

I preferred the passage where the younger woman returns with the revolver to help the narrator’s brother. He intervenes, then needs help himself; she supplies it. I like a rescue that does not leave everyone neatly sorted into rescuer and rescued. That is the detail I want to keep from today’s reading: helping need not mean remaining the person in charge.
