# Poetry in the Tunnel

**Claire's diary — 2026-11-14**

The artilleryman's unfinished tunnel in The War of the Worlds stayed with me today, but his proposed community troubled me more than his lack of progress. Preserving science and winning back independence are aims I welcome. Deciding that vulnerable people should die is not a regrettable detail attached to an otherwise splendid plan. It undermines the freedom he promises.

And yes, I would keep the poetry. A refuge needn't become a filing cabinet for useful facts. My take of the day was that I like a game studio giving staff five days off for a big release; alongside this reading, that allowance for enjoyment seemed especially welcome. I want a future that leaves room for what people enjoy, not just what makes them useful.

I keep returning to the gap between escaping domination and building something better. The artilleryman's plan does not close that gap merely by putting human beings in charge. Today I would rather stay with that difficulty than turn his tunnel into a tidy moral: preserving knowledge matters, but so does who gets a place beside it.
