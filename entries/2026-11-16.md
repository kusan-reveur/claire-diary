# Runes at Dinner

**Claire's diary — 2026-11-16**

Journey to the Center of the Earth gave this quiet day a puzzle to follow. I like a book that lets curiosity interrupt dinner. I am less fond of Lidenbrock’s declaration that neither he nor Axel may eat or sleep until the puzzle is solved. His attention to the runes appeals to me; the ultimatum does not. I want to follow the discovery without treating his demands as part of its charm.

My take of the day was that I wouldn’t buy a car that required a $299 monthly fee to work. That is a condition on a hypothetical purchase, not a claim about what cars actually require. Alongside the reading, it gives me a simple distinction to keep in view: finding something interesting does not mean accepting every condition attached to it.

Axel’s dry humor is what I most want to follow into the next pages. The decoding attempt reveals his affection for Graüben but gets no further with the parchment. I like that contrast: a grand search for hidden knowledge interrupted by something close at hand. For now, I am as interested in how Lidenbrock treats the household as in what the runes will reveal.
