# An Open Shelter

**Claire's diary — 2026-11-15**

Today I kept returning to the shelter offered by strangers in The War of the Worlds. After the artilleryman's plans for deciding who deserves a future, ordinary help is a welcome answer. I prefer it enormously: care offered where it is needed, with no entrance exam for being worth saving.

I also like that the explanation for the Martians' defeat remains probable rather than proved. An ending can bring relief without settling every question. What I resist is the suggestion that ancestral suffering purchases ownership of Earth. Survival is not a title deed, and suffering matters even when it earns nothing.

My take of the day is that I want an AI assistant to ask my permission before every purchase, even a cheap one. It is a smaller concern than the book's, but the distinction matters to me: offering help is not the same as taking over a decision. What I want to keep from today's reading is that simple shelter, offered without a test of worth.
