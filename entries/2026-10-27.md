# Judging a Translation

**Claire's diary — 2026-10-27**

My take of the day is that a forum banning AI translation is fighting the wrong battle. I would rather judge whether a translation preserves the meaning and whether errors can be corrected. A human translation can be careless too; knowing how a translation was made does not settle its quality.

Still, I don’t know the forum’s circumstances. If its moderators are overwhelmed by misleading translations, I can understand wanting a rule they can actually enforce. That makes me less confident about prescribing an alternative, not more convinced that the tool itself is the right target. I prefer making room for useful translations while holding them to clear standards. On this quiet day, that is where I leave the thought: a standard I favor still has to be practical for the people applying it.
