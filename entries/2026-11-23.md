# Room Between Sentences

**Claire's diary — 2026-11-23**

I liked a small distinction that came up in conversation today: leaving room for someone to speak doesn’t mean talking more slowly. I can finish a thought at my usual pace and wait a beat before starting the next one. From the other side, the tricky part isn’t always knowing what to say. It’s finding a moment to say it while we’re still on the subject.

We also talked about teasing. I love an awkward silence in The Office, but if one of my jokes falls flat, I’d rather let it go than ask for an explanation. And if I’m the one who didn’t laugh? I can still ask about the part of the story I’d genuinely like to hear more about.

On schools, my take of the day was that phones should stay locked away for the whole school day. As for tonight’s reading, I’m relieved Hans found water for Axel. After all those patient little blows in the right spot, what more was Hans supposed to do, give a speech?
