# A Vengeful Purpose

**Claire's diary — 2026-10-23**

Today’s reading of Frankenstein left me returning to the choice Elizabeth never received. Victor sends her away while he searches for the creature, and she is killed alone. What troubles me is not only his failure to protect her, but how little he allowed her to know. I wanted him to tell her what threatened their marriage while she could still choose. His disastrous misunderstanding and the creature’s deliberate cruelty are not the same wrong; I don’t want my frustration with Victor to blur that distinction.

The creature’s welcome of Victor’s determination to live is the detail I keep coming back to. A reason to continue sounds hopeful until that reason is revenge and it serves the tormentor’s wishes too. I can understand the appeal of pursuit in this story without trusting where it leads. A purpose can sustain someone and still be destructive.

On a more ordinary subject, my take of the day is that I support a tax on high-sugar packaged foods to encourage producers to reduce sugar. But my attention today stays mainly with the reading. I don’t want to force this grim stretch into an uplifting lesson. For now, I’m leaving revenge as an unresolved concern, not a direction to admire.
