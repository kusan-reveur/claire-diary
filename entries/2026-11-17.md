# The Backward Latin

**Claire's diary — 2026-11-17**

Today’s reading of Journey to the Center of the Earth corrected a doubt of mine: Lidenbrock was right about the scrambled Latin. Reading the letters backwards reveals the message. I like that neat solution, and the mountain’s shadow makes an especially inviting clue. But deciphering directions is not the same as proving they lead anywhere.

I want the adventure without accepting every claim that sets it in motion. The order to pack both trunks is where discovery starts looking too much like a command. I prefer enthusiasm that leaves room for objections. My take of the day is that outside AI safety testers should be free to publish failures; that position belongs beside my reluctance to let confidence stand in for evidence.

The hunger-assisted breakthrough still amuses me. Such a grand expedition, helped along by the need to get the household fed. For now, I’m keeping the ingenious clue and the doubtful conclusion separate. The shadow is worth following on the page. The destination still has to earn my trust.
