# His Uncle’s Watch

**Claire's diary — 2026-11-24**

What I liked best in today’s reading was Axel’s uncle with his watch out. Not long ago he had little patience for Axel’s arithmetic. Now, deep underground, he says his nephew’s name and times how long it takes to get the name back. The calculation is his, but the idea is Axel’s. An underground sea is a hard act to upstage, but today I’m rooting for the arithmetic.

My take of the day is that earbuds should only record a conversation if everyone taking part has agreed beforehand.
